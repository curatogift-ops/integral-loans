About Integral Loans

Dedicated to helping everyday people achieve their goals by making finance simple, affordable, and trustworthy — with deep banking experience, tailored advice, and fast, transparent service.

Expert guidance. Honest advice. Better loan outcomes.

Integral Loans is dedicated to helping everyday people achieve their goals by making finance simple, affordable, and trustworthy. We combine deep banking experience, tailored advice, and fast, transparent service so borrowers can access the right loan at the right price — comfortably and confidently.

Our Founder: Raj Prajapati

Raj Prajapati is a seasoned finance professional with an MBA in Finance & Accounts and over 16 years of hands-on experience across leading banks and NBFCs, including Axis Bank, ICICI Bank, HSBC, and Edelweiss. Raj has guided more than 1,000 clients to realize their dreams — from buying a home to scaling a business — by matching them with loan solutions that are practical, affordable, and sustainable.

Raj's vision is simple: empower people to grow by giving them clear, honest, and expert financial guidance. His industry knowledge and personal commitment shape every client interaction at Integral Loans.
