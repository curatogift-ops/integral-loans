Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: May 2026

Integral Loans respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use personal information.

Information We Collect

  • Name, phone number, email, and loan requirements submitted via forms
  • Technical data such as browser type and pages visited (via standard analytics, if enabled)

How We Use Information

We use your data to respond to inquiries, process loan applications with partner lenders, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Sharing

Information may be shared with banks and NBFCs only as needed to evaluate or process your loan request, and with service providers who assist our operations under confidentiality obligations.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at info@integralloans.com.
