Who Can Apply for a Home Loan?

Home loan eligibility may vary depending on the lender, but generally, applicants may include:

  • Salaried professionals
  • Self-employed individuals
  • Business owners
  • First-time homebuyers
  • Property buyers looking to purchase under construction or ready property

Common factors considered: Age, Income, Credit profile, Employment stability, Existing obligations, Property details.

Documents Usually Required

Identity & Address

  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar card
  • Passport
  • Voter ID
  • Driving license

Income Proof

  • Salary slips
  • Bank statements
  • ITR / Form 16
  • Business financials

Property

  • Agreement to sale
  • Property papers
  • Approved plan
  • NOC & related docs

Other

  • Passport-size photos
  • Application form
  • Lender-specific documents
